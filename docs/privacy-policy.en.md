**From {{ORGANISATION}}'s directory**, for {{ORGANISATION}} staff only: membership of

**From you or from event organisers:**

## 6. Purpose of the processing

To provide the service: authentication and access control, organising and publishing meetings
and events, handling registrations, and sending notifications. Logs are used for security and
troubleshooting.

**Legal basis:** {{LEGAL_BASIS}}
*[For a Swedish public university: performance of a task carried out in the public interest,
Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR.]*

## 7. Who can see your data

- **Logged-in users** can search for other users. A search result shows name, title, e-mail
  address, affiliation and profile picture. Profile pages themselves are visible only to their
  owner and to administrators.
  *[Describe your setting here: Indico's `ALLOW_PUBLIC_USER_SEARCH`.]*
- **People listed in an event:** speakers, chairs and authors are shown with their name and
  affiliation to everyone who can see the event.
- **Participant lists** are shown only if the organiser publishes them. Where the organiser asks
  for consent, only for registrants who agree.
- **Event managers** see the registrations for their events.
- **Protected categories and events** are visible only to the people and groups granted access.
- **Administrators** of the service can see all data.

## 9. Retention

- **Event content** (agendas, speakers, minutes, material) is kept as long as the event exists.
  It forms part of {{ORGANISATION}}'s record of its activities, subject to
  {{RECORDS_RULES}}. *[Name the archiving rules that apply, or state that they are under review.]*
- **Registrations:** each registration form has a retention period set by the organiser, at most
  {{MAX_REGISTRATION_RETENTION}}. When it expires, the registrations are deleted.
- **Accounts:**
  - an account with no login for {{BLOCK_AFTER}} is blocked: it cannot log in, is hidden from
    search, and is kept so it can be reactivated;
  - after {{ANONYMISE_AFTER}} without login, the account is anonymised.
  - Your name stays on agendas where you appeared as a speaker or author unless you ask for it to
    be removed.
- **Web server logs:** {{LOG_RETENTION}}.
- **Backups:** {{BACKUP_RETENTION}}. Deleted data disappears from backups when they expire.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to:
- access your data, and to export it yourself (*My profile → Data export*);
- have it corrected or erased;
- object to processing;
- complain to the supervisory authority.

Send requests to {{REQUESTS_CONTACT}}. Some content may have to be kept under the public records
and archiving rules that apply to {{ORGANISATION}}.
